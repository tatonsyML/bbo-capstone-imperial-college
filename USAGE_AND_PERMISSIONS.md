## Data sources and reproducibility

The [full dataset](data/all_observations.csv) distinguishes the **175 starting observations** from **104 personal portal evaluations**. A reconstructed mathematical surface or retrospective optimiser suggestion is not a new portal evaluation. See the [datasheet](DATASHEET.md) and [optimisation methods](docs/methods.md) for methodology and limitations.
