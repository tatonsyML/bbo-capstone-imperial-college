This is a **source-to-publication cross-check** of all thirteen sections in the author's original, **52-page _Capstone Project.docx_**, compared with the corresponding [round history](../journey/README.md), [function narratives](../functions/README.md) and recorded Python/evaluation evidence. The private working document was uploaded to the author's ChatGPT library on **8 November 2026**. It is **not redistributed** in this repository.

The original discussion posts are **first-person evidence of methods, intentions and reflections**. They are *not* automatically executable code, authenticated timestamps for a Python run, or proof that a particular candidate was generated by a particular script. Where an original submission and the portal record diverge, **the portal input and return take priority**, and the mismatch stays visible.
