# Round 13 addendum to Component 25.1

**Prepared after the original retrospective.** The final portal results were not available when the retrospective was drafted. This addendum records them separately so that prior reasoning is not rewritten after observing the outcomes.

Three new personal bests emerged in Round 13: **F2 0.7442504263021256**, **F3 −0.0037530969187192596** and **F4 0.7468979113807426**. F1, F5 and F8 exactly repeated earlier scores, F7 was lower than its previous best by only 4.38 × 10⁻¹³ at a nearly identical input, and F6 returned below its R12 best.

The results strengthen the argument against equating a plateau with convergence. F2 found a significantly more productive region, F4 improved beyond its earlier wider-search breakthrough, and F3 improved despite the dwindling evaluation budget. These outcomes do not prove that any single method caused the gain or retrospectively authenticate an unrecorded forecast.
