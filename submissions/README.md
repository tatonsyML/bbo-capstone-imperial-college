## How this archive differs from the current documents

The [main README](../README.md) gives final verified results through R13. Some archived notes were written when later rounds were pending or earlier models appeared more credible. They remain useful as evidence of the *decision at the time*, not claims about today's state. Where an exact original artefact was not retained, the folder indicates the scope of the recovered material rather than fabricating a complete notebook or result.
