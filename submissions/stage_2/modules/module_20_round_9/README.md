# Capstone BBO — Module 20 / Round 9

The package preserves the project history before the Round 9 portal results exist.

- `initial_data/` contains the original `.npy` arrays.
- `submission_history_through_week8.csv` records every observed portal result.
- `week8_audit.csv` compares the Week 8 return with forecasts recorded before the result.
- `scaling_ledger.csv` records incumbent gains round by round.
- `round9_queries.csv` records the proposed Round 9 queries, rationales and prospective forecasts.
- `round9_portal_values.txt` contains the six-decimal strings for the portal.
- `round9_controlled_fits.py` reproduces the simple local fits for F2, F3, F4, F5 and F7.

No Round 9 result is included. This keeps prediction and later explanation separate.
